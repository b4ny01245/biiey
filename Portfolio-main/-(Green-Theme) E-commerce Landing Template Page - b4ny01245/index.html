Started at a kitchen table, still run that way

Grove began in 2019 when our founder got tired of replacing cheap homeware every year. We now work with a small group of makers across the Philippines to produce goods that are meant to be used daily and repaired, not replaced.

"We'd rather sell you one good tote than five bad ones."

Every material is chosen for how it ages — canvas that softens, stoneware that survives the dishwasher, wool that holds up to a decade of winters. If something breaks, we fix it.
